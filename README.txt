ONLY YOU, PIHU — ONE MONTH ANNIVERSARY WEBSITE

1. Open index.html in Chrome to preview.
2. Add your 15 photos in the memories folder, named 01.jpg, 02.jpg, ... 15.jpg.
   If your photos are PNG/HEIC, convert to JPG first.
3. (Optional) Add your own licensed song as music.mp3 beside index.html.
4. (Optional) Add your own edited video as our-movie.mp4 beside index.html.
5. Open index.html in a text editor to customize:
   - CONFIG.startDate (currently 2026-09-13, PLEASE CONFIRM THE REAL DATE)
   - captions, notes, reasons, and letters
   - ending signature if needed.
6. To share it online, upload the entire folder to a static web host such as Netlify or GitHub Pages.

The site works without photos/music/video, showing beautiful placeholders.
No images, audio, or video of your relationship are included until you add them.
